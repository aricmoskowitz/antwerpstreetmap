# Antwerp Inside the Ring — Learning Curriculum

*A three-tier curriculum for learning the streets, squares, waterways, buildings, and parks of Antwerp's historic core, built from the reference map. Numbering follows Super Section.Section.Module (e.g. 1.1.1). An object may appear in more than one module — a long boulevard, for instance, belongs both to the city-wide roads module and to every neighborhood it passes through. Super Section 1 uses curated, filtered lists to keep the foundations manageable; the complete, unfiltered lists of every category live in Super Section 8.*
